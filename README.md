🚀 TwinAI – AI-Powered Communication Intelligence Platform:-

An intelligent communication coaching platform that leverages Artificial Intelligence, Speech Recognition, and Large Language Models to evaluate, analyze, and improve communication skills through presentations, interviews, debates, and document-based assessments.

📖 Overview:-

TwinAI is an AI-powered Communication Intelligence Platform designed to help students, professionals, and job seekers enhance their communication skills through personalized AI-driven feedback.

The platform goes beyond traditional speech-to-text systems by analyzing content quality, confidence, fluency, clarity, vocabulary, interview performance, debate skills, and presentation effectiveness.

TwinAI combines modern AI technologies such as Groq Whisper, Groq GPT-OSS-120B, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Communication Analytics to deliver comprehensive communication evaluations and actionable improvement recommendations.

🎯 Problem Statement:-

Communication plays a critical role in academic success, interviews, presentations, and professional careers. However, many individuals struggle with:

Public speaking anxiety
Lack of communication confidence
Ineffective presentation skills
Poor interview performance
Limited access to communication mentors
Subjective and inconsistent feedback

TwinAI addresses these challenges by providing automated, objective, and personalized communication coaching powered by AI.
